## Uplinx 2.0.1

- The real Uplinx logo — the flat, symmetric "u" from the app icon — now sits centred in its container at the top of the side panel, in your theme's colours.
- **Add servers** button on the home screen (it used to be the "+" at the top of the side panel).
- The title bar shows only the name and the connection status.

**Downloads**
- `Uplinx-2.0.1.msi` — installs for the current user, no admin rights needed.
- `Uplinx-2.0.1-portable-win64.zip` — unpack anywhere and run `Uplinx.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` — checksums.
