## Uplinx 1.6.1

**Download:** `Uplinx-1.6.1-setup.exe` (installer), `Uplinx-1.6.1.msi`, or `Uplinx-1.6.1-portable-win64.zip`.

### New flat logo
- The **u** is now **flat**: no extruded shadow or gloss, just a clean primary → tertiary gradient.
- **No background:** the installer, window, tray and title-bar icons are the letter alone, perfectly symmetric and centred.
- The navigation rail keeps its turning, morphing Material You container, with the flat letter inside.
- In the app, the logo still recolours itself with your theme and accent.

### Everything from 1.6.0
- Own title bar with live connection status.
- Split tunnelling (only chosen apps / all except chosen).
- Speed test of any server, with a speedometer widget.
- Windows notifications.
